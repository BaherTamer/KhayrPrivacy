Privacy Policy

Khayr keeps your tasks yours. No accounts, no tracking, no servers of ours.

What Khayr Stores

Your tasks, routines, reminders, and preferences are stored on your device. Khayr has no user accounts and no sign-in, and it never asks for personal information.

iCloud Sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, Khayr keeps your tasks in sync across your devices using Apple's CloudKit. Your data is stored in your own private iCloud database, tied to your Apple Account. It is not sent to any server operated by Khayr, and the developer of Khayr cannot read it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

You can turn syncing off at any time in Settings › your name › iCloud. With sync off, Khayr keeps working and your tasks stay on the device.

Notifications

Notifications are scheduled and delivered locally by your device. Their contents are not transmitted anywhere.

Data Sharing & Third Parties

Khayr contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind. It bundles no third-party code — only Apple's own system frameworks. Nothing about you or your usage is shared with anyone.

Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete any task at any time inside the app. Deleting a task removes it from your device, and from iCloud on all your devices if syncing is on. Deleting Khayr removes its local data; to remove synced data as well, use Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as Khayr gains new features. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
