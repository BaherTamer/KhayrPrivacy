Privacy Policy

Khayr is designed to work fully on-device. Your tasks, preferences, and reminders are stored locally on your device.

Data Sharing & Third Parties

Khayr does not include any accounts, sign-ins, tracking, or analytics. The app uses Apple system frameworks to run locally on your device.

Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete tasks at any time inside the app. Deleted tasks are removed from local storage.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Khayr gains new features. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
